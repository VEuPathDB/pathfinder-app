# A study that is not in the catalog is searched for thirty-seven times

**What I did.** On vectorbase in the web app, a new conversation: "Open the Anopheles gambiae hemocyte study after Plasmodium infection in the EDA (the Pinto or Baton microarray), filter to infected versus control hemocytes, preview the samples, and run a volcano plot of infected versus control. Then tell me what the top hits mean." Thread `83feffce-baa9-4357-b160-cb226b0f5120`. The site's EDA catalog holds no hemocyte microarray: the top 100 of `search_eda_studies("Anopheles gambiae hemocytes")` are RNA-seq, small-RNA and single-cell studies.

**What I got.** 710 s, 69 tool calls, 2M tokens, $0.99, then the reply "I could not safely start the requested analysis because the site's study catalog does not currently expose the Pinto/Baton hemocyte microarray ... Would you like me to proceed with the related 48-hour hemocyte RNA-seq study as a substitute". The trail: `search_eda_studies` 37 times (limits 5 to 100, queries from author names to a GEO accession found on the web), `research_web_search` 21 times (hunting the accession), `research_literature_search` 7 times, `describe_eda_study` twice. The 37 searches returned 29 distinct top-3 sets; the best relevance ranged 0.22 to 0.76; every call's summary read "N studies matched <query>".

**Why that's wrong.** The answer is the right one and it was available after the first search: the catalog is a few hundred studies, the ranking walks all of them, and nothing the model can type changes what is in it. Twelve minutes and a dollar went to reformulation, on a turn whose useful work was one question.

**Why it happens.** `ai/tools/standalone/eda_catalog.py::search_eda_studies` reports a semantic top-k as "N studies matched", so a study that is not on the site never reads as absent (the "No study matched" branch is reachable only with an empty catalog: `services/eda/catalog.py::search_studies` walks the whole index and always fills `limit`), and nothing bounds how often the Lead reads one discovery tool in a turn: `LEAD_USAGE_LIMITS` is 80 calls of anything, and `ToolRepetitionGuard` refuses identical arguments only, with `search_eda_studies` and `describe_eda_study` not even in `READ_ONLY_TOOLS`.

**Fix.** Two parts, each in the repository that owns it. In the app, the study search answers as what it is: "the K closest of M studies on this site (best match 0.72)", and its guidance says that a study named by author, title or accession that is not among them is not on this site, so the next step is to offer the closest or ask, not to search again; `search_eda_studies` and `describe_eda_study` join `READ_ONLY_TOOLS`. In the runtime, the repetition guard grows a per-run cap per tool name (a vocabulary the product supplies, as `READ_ONLY_TOOLS` is): the call past the cap is answered with a refusal that names the count and says to report what the catalog holds, and the cap is a run-level fact the ledger records, as a sub-agent's budget stop is. PathFinder names the caps for its discovery tools (study search, web search, literature search). Red first: a mock Lead turn that calls `search_eda_studies` past the cap with a fresh query each time is refused on the call past the cap and reports the closest study; the tool's summary on a full catalog never says "matched".

**What you'd get.** The same reply after one or two searches: the study is not on this site, here is the closest, which do you want.
